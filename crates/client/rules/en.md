# Snook Legend: the rules of eight-ball, shown in the game.
# "## <part>" starts a part, "### <title>" a rule, and the lines after it
# are its text. The same parts and rules, in the same order, in every
# language.

## The game

### The aim
Each player has a group of seven balls: the solids (1 to 7) or the stripes (9 to 15). Pocket all of yours, then the 8-ball, before your opponent does.

### Your turn
You keep shooting while you pocket a ball of your group on a legal shot. Miss, foul or pocket one of your opponent's balls, and the turn passes.

## The break

### Breaking
The cue ball starts behind the head string. A legal break pockets a ball or drives at least four object balls to a cushion; otherwise it is a foul, and the opponent gets the cue ball in hand behind the head string.

### After the break
If you make a ball on a legal break, the first one made decides your group and you shoot again. The 8-ball made on the break is put back on the foot spot: no one loses.

### The open table
Until someone's group is decided the table is open: you may hit any ball first except the 8. The first ball you pocket on a legal shot gives you its group, and your opponent the other.

## Legal shots and fouls

### A legal shot
The cue ball must hit a ball of your group first (the 8 once your group is cleared). Then a ball must be pocketed, or some ball must reach a cushion.

### Fouls
Hitting nothing, hitting the wrong ball first, no ball reaching a cushion after contact, pocketing the cue ball (a scratch), or sending any ball off the table.

### Ball in hand
After a foul your opponent gets the cue ball in hand and can place it anywhere on the table (behind the head string after a foul on the break). A ball that left the table goes back on the foot spot.

### Your opponent's ball
Pocketing one of your opponent's balls passes the turn, even if one of yours went down too. It is not a foul: no ball in hand.

### The shot clock
When a game has a shot clock and it runs out, the turn passes and your opponent gets ball in hand.

## The 8-ball

### Calling the pocket
Once your group is cleared, choose the pocket for the 8 before you shoot. Pocket it there on a legal shot and you win.

### Losing on the 8
You lose if you pocket the 8 before your group is cleared, in a pocket other than the one called, on a foul (a scratch included), or if you knock it off the table.
