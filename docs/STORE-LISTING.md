# Store listing (Microsoft Store)

Texts for Partner Center → the product → **Store listings**, one block per
language. Limits: short description 1,000 characters, description 10,000,
"What's new in this version" 1,500, up to 20 product features of 200
characters each, up to 7 search terms.

The screenshots for each language are in `store-art/screenshots/<language>/`
(1920 × 1080, in the order to upload them); the logos and the hero art are in
`store-art/` (see `tools/store_art.py`).

**Coin purchases are off** in this version (the server isn't set up for the
Store yet, see [MSSTORE.md](MSSTORE.md)): the texts below don't mention
buying anything, and the age rating questionnaire shouldn't either. When the
Store sale is turned on, add the block at the end of this file to each
description and tick "in-game purchases" in the questionnaire.

## English

### Short description

3D eight-ball pool with real physics on an 8-foot table. Play rated games online, take on fifteen opponents with real personalities, beat the daily challenge and level up. Free.

### Description

Snook Legend is a 3D eight-ball pool game for people who like to really play: every shot follows the laws of physics, the balls roll like real ones and the side pockets are open, so skill counts for more than luck.

Full control of the cue: aim, choose the power, the exact point where the tip strikes the cue ball and the cue's elevation, which you raise by dragging it in a side view of the shot. No numbers on screen: you judge the power and the elevation by eye, as on a real table. Draw, follow, side spin, jump shots and massé.

ONLINE
Walk into the lounge, chat, invite someone or use "Play now" to find an opponent at your level. Rated or casual games, open tables (public or with a password), 4, 8 or 16-player tournaments with a live bracket, friends, daily games with up to 3 days per turn and the world ranking. Pick one of 32 profile pictures and your country.

AGAINST THE COMPUTER
Fifteen opponents with a style and personality of their own, from Leo, who is still learning, to Viktor, the champion, by way of Tito, who bets everything on every shot, and Yuri, who never misses. They talk about the game as they play (you can turn that off). Beat all fifteen on the Tour to earn an exclusive cue, or take on a friend's "ghost", which plays in their style.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
The daily challenge gives everyone the same position, with a ranking for the day. Drills with stars measure your potting, your spin and your banks. Every game earns experience for your account level.

YOUR STYLE
Cues, cloths, chalk, ball sets, themed tables and halls, all earned with the coins you win by playing. Your cues earn rings and inlays as you win with them.

MADE TO SEE CLEARLY
An evenly lit table, balls whose numbers always read and soft shadows. A score panel that shows each player's balls in the order they went down. Rather play from above? An option locks the camera to the top view. Runs on practically any PC, from old laptops to gaming rigs, with graphics from Low to Ultra.

The rules of the game are a click away, in the menu and during a game.

In English, Portuguese and Spanish.

### What's new in this version

Version 0.4.0: the game is now called Snook Legend!
• An 8-foot table with much more open side pockets: a ball off the centre or coming in at an angle now drops. The balls roll a little more freely.
• New rule: making one of your opponent's balls passes the turn, even if one of yours goes down too.
• A new score panel: pictures, names and each player's balls rolling into place, in the order they went down.
• Aim by eye: raise the cue by dragging it in a side view; no more numbers on the power or the elevation.
• 32 profile pictures to choose from.
• The rules of the game in the menu and in the pause menu.
• A "Top view only" option that locks the camera.
• Easier to see: an evenly lit table, readable ball numbers and lighter shadows.
• Five new opponents (Tito, Inês, Diego, Amara and Yuri) and a fifteen-stage Tour; your earlier wins still count. Their remarks can be turned off.
• Replays and daily games that started before keep the old rules until they end.
