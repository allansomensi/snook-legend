Controle total do taco: mire, escolha a força, o ponto exato em que a ponta bate na branca e a inclinação do taco. Puxe, siga, dê efeito lateral, pule bolas e faça massé.

ONLINE

TODO DIA TEM ALGO NOVO
O desafio diário traz a mesma posição para todos, com ranking do dia. Os treinos com estrelas medem sua caçapa, seu efeito e suas tabelas. Cada partida dá experiência para o nível da sua conta.

SEU ESTILO
Tacos, panos, giz, jogos de bolas, mesas e salões temáticos. Seus tacos ganham anéis e incrustações conforme você vence com eles.

LEVE E BONITO
Roda em praticamente qualquer PC, de notebooks antigos a máquinas gamer, com gráficos de Baixo a Ultra (sombras suaves, reflexos no piso e salões detalhados).

## English

3D eight-ball pool with real physics on a regulation 9-foot table. Play rated games online, take on ten opponents with real personalities, beat the daily challenge and level up. Free.

### Description

Snookered is a 3D eight-ball pool game for people who like to really play: the table has official tournament measurements, the balls weigh what real ones do, and every shot follows the laws of physics.

Full cue control: aim, set the power, pick the exact point where the tip strikes the cue ball and raise the cue. Draw, follow, side spin, jump shots and massé.

ONLINE
Walk into the lounge, chat, invite someone or hit "Play now" to find an opponent at your level. Rated or casual games, open tables (public or with a password), 4, 8 or 16-player tournaments with a live bracket, friends, daily games with up to 3 days a turn, and the world ranking.

AGAINST THE COMPUTER
Ten opponents with their own style and personality, from Leo, who is still learning, to Viktor, the champion. They talk about the game as they play. Beat all ten on the Tour to win an exclusive cue, or challenge a friend's "ghost", which plays in their style.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
The daily challenge gives everyone the same position, with a ranking of the day. Drills with stars test your potting, spin and banks. Every game earns experience for your account level.

YOUR STYLE
Cues, cloths, chalk, ball sets, themed tables and halls. Your cues earn rings and inlays as you win with them.

LIGHT AND GOOD-LOOKING
Runs on practically any PC, from old laptops to gaming rigs, with graphics from Low to Ultra (soft shadows, reflective floors and detailed halls).

In English, Portuguese and Spanish.
