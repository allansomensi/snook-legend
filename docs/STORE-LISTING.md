## English

### Short description

3D eight-ball pool with real physics on an 8-foot table. Play rated games online, take on fifteen opponents with real personalities, beat the daily challenge and level up. Free, with optional coin purchases.

### Description

Snook Legend is a 3D eight-ball pool game for people who like to really play: every shot follows the laws of physics, the balls roll like real ones and the side pockets are open, so skill counts for more than luck.

Full control of the cue: aim, choose the power, the exact point where the tip strikes the cue ball and the cue's elevation. Draw, follow, side spin, jump shots and massé.

ONLINE
Walk into the lounge, chat, invite someone or use "Play now" to find an opponent at your level. Rated or casual games, open tables (public or with a password), 4, 8 or 16-player tournaments with a live bracket, friends, daily games with up to 3 days per turn and the world ranking.

AGAINST THE COMPUTER
Fifteen opponents with a style and personality of their own, from Leo, who is still learning, to Viktor, the champion, by way of Tito, who bets everything on every shot, and Yuri, who never misses. They talk about the game as they play (you can turn that off). Beat all fifteen on the Tour to earn an exclusive cue, or take on a friend's "ghost", which plays in their style.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
The daily challenge gives everyone the same position, with a ranking for the day. Drills with stars measure your potting, your spin and your banks. Every game earns experience for your account level.

YOUR STYLE
Cues, cloths, chalk, ball sets, themed tables and halls. Your cues earn rings and inlays as you win with them.

MADE TO SEE CLEARLY
An evenly lit table, balls whose numbers always read and soft shadows. Runs on practically any PC, from old laptops to gaming rigs, with graphics from Low to Ultra.

IN-GAME PURCHASES
The game is free. Coins come from playing (rated games and a bonus for every day you play) and can also be bought, if you like, in packs from US$ X to US$ Y. They are only for cosmetic items: nothing that wins games.

In English, Portuguese and Spanish.

### What's new in this version

Version 0.4.0
• An 8-foot table with much more open side pockets: a ball off the centre or coming in at an angle now drops.
• The balls roll a little more freely.
• New rule: making one of your opponent's balls passes the turn, even if one of yours goes down too.
• Easier to see: an evenly lit table, balls with a soft sheen and readable numbers, lighter shadows, and nothing of the hall in the way of the view from above.
• Five new opponents (Tito, Inês, Diego, Amara and Yuri) and a fifteen-stage Tour; your earlier wins still count.
• An option to turn the opponents' remarks off.
• Coin packs sold through the Microsoft Store itself.
• Replays and daily games that started before keep the old rules until they end.
